### Fee explainer (canonical for support)
**Brokerage & trade charges: what to know**
- Brokerage is the fee the broker charges for each executed equity order; Groww publishes its current rates on its pricing page. [1]
- Brokerage is not the only cost: statutory charges such as STT, exchange transaction charges, GST, SEBI turnover fees and stamp duty are added by regulation and are not set by the broker. [1]
- Selling delivery shares also attracts depository (DP) charges, applied per sell order, not per stock. [1]
- Charges apply per executed order, so splitting one trade into several orders can increase the total fee. [1]
- Groww's Help Centre lists the charges that appear in a delivery trade report: brokerage, DP charges, stamp duty, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charges, the investor protection fund charge, STT and GST. [2]
- Groww's delivery brokerage is the lower of a flat fee or a percentage of the order value, subject to a minimum; the exact figures are on the pricing page. [1]
